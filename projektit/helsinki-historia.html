Kaupungin historialliset kartat

The Senate Atlas is a series of maps of Southern Finland drawn by the Russian Army topographic troops in the end of the 19th and the beginning of the 20th centuries in scale 1:21 000, mapping out the southern parts of Finland. As Finland participated in the mapping costs for 102 000 rubles (Strang, 2014) they were given two pieces of each map sheet that were distributed to the governmental branche of land surveying (Maanmittausylihallitus) and the war-time committee of the Senate (sota-asiaintoimituskunta). Originally black-and-white this resulted in two different colorings. Sources: J. Strang 2014, Venäjän Suomi-kuva – Venäjä Suomen kartoittajana 1710–1942 The WMS map tiles are published by Helsinki Region Infoshare. OpenStreetMap.
